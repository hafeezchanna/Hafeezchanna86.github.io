From the warehouse floor to the systems that run it

Most people who build inventory software have never counted a pallet at 6am or explained a stock variance to a director. I have. For over a decade I managed complete warehouse operations for Liberty Agro Chemicals — safe pesticide and chemical storage, FIFO and FEFO rotation to stop expiry losses, stock audits, dispatch coordination, and a team to supervise and train.

That experience is the reason my technical work is useful. I know what a stockout actually costs, why a dead-stock report gets ignored, and what a storekeeper will and won't type into a form. I now build in Python — Streamlit dashboards, Pandas and Plotly analysis, SQLite-backed desktop applications, and AI assistants using the Claude API — and I aim every one of them at a real operational problem.

I am looking for a role as an AI/ML engineer, data analyst, or Python developer where domain knowledge of supply chain and warehousing is an advantage rather than a footnote.

AI Warehouse Assistant

Supply chain dashboard

Labour Attendance & Payroll System
